Chapter Thirteen — The Jeep

Ryo Kurosaki had never been assigned a roommate, and he considered it the single greatest administrative gift of his life.

The room was his, all of it, and it stayed the way he liked it — which was to say, barely touched. A bed he made when he felt like it. A desk he mostly used as a footrest. A single good speaker in the corner playing something with a slow bassline, the kind of music that didn't ask anything of you. He was tipped back in his chair now, feet up on the desk, eyes half-closed, letting the afternoon happen somewhere on the other side of the window without his participation.

School was a chore. He'd decided that a long time ago and saw no reason to revisit the ruling. He did the minimum, he skated the surface, and it bored him in a way he found almost restful. He had a car in the campus lot with a full tank and no particular attachment to this hill; he could get in it any afternoon he chose and drive until the city ran out and never look back. He thought about that sometimes. The freedom of it.

He never did it. He preferred, for reasons he didn't examine too closely, to stay exactly where he was. Where certain people were.

His phone buzzed against the desk.

He cracked one eye, saw the name, and let out a low grunt that was half annoyance and half something he'd have denied under oath. Women, he thought. School was one chore. Women were another, apparently — a newer one, louder, and as far as he could tell, entirely without an off switch.

yuki: you bum. meet me outside. rins gone and im bored.

He typed with one thumb, unhurried.

ryo: i'd rather do almost anything else.

yuki: no you wouldnt

He looked at that for a second. It was annoying, mostly because it was true. He set the phone down without answering, on principle, and closed his eye again and tried to sink back into the bassline.

The phone buzzed twice more.

yuki: courtyard. now, big boy.

He sighed, long and put-upon, at the ceiling. Then he swung his feet off the desk.

ryo: be right there.

He grabbed his jacket off the back of the chair, killed the speaker, and paused a moment at the door, thinking — with the low, permanent hum of worry he'd carried since he was fifteen — that he hoped, wherever she was, Rin was having an easier afternoon than he was about to.
